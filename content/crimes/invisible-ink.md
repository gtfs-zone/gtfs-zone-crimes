---
title: Invisible ink
tagline: White text on a white route.
---

`route_color` paints the badge and `route_text_color` writes the route name on
it. When they are the same colour, or close enough, nobody can read the badge.
The spec even says it: the text colour must contrast with the route colour.

## The law

A route sets both colours, and the contrast between them is below 1.5:1
(the WCAG minimum for any text is 4.5:1).

## How to get pardoned

Pick a text colour that can be read on the route colour. When in doubt, black
or white, whichever contrasts more.
