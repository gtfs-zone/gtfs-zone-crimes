---
title: Time travel
tagline: Arrives before it leaves.
---

Within a trip, time goes forwards. A vehicle that reaches the next stop before
it leaves this one, or leaves a stop before it gets there, is either a time
machine or an export bug. Usually it is a trip past midnight written as 00:10
instead of 24:10.

## The law

A trip where a stop's arrival is earlier than the previous stop's departure,
or a stop's departure is earlier than its own arrival.

Times past 24:00:00 are fine, and so are times past 48:00:00: Amtrak's long
distance trains legitimately run that long.

Only trips whose service still runs are judged.

## How to get pardoned

Times count from the start of the service day. A trip that starts at 23:50 and
runs past midnight goes 23:50, 24:05, 24:20.
