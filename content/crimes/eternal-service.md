---
title: Eternal service
tagline: Service until 2099.
---

A feed should say when it stops being true. A calendar that runs until 2099
says "forever", which means a trip planner will keep showing this timetable
long after the routes change, and nobody can tell an outdated feed from a
current one.

## The law

The last service date is more than five years from today.

## How to get pardoned

End the calendar at the end of the timetable period, and publish a new feed
when the next one is ready.
