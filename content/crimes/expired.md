The most basic crime of all: a feed whose last day of service has already
passed. Every trip planner using it shows no service at all, or keeps showing
the old one.

## The law

The last service date in calendar.txt and calendar_dates.txt is before today.

## How to get pardoned

Publish the current timetable.
