---
title: Dead link
tagline: The URL in every catalog that goes nowhere.
---

A feed is only as good as its URL. These are listed in the public catalogs,
copied into apps and scripts, and do not answer.

## The law

The schedule URL has never answered a check, or has been down for 30 days in a
row.

## How to get pardoned

Bring the URL back, or tell the [Mobility Database](https://mobilitydatabase.org)
and the [Transitland Atlas](https://github.com/transitland/transitland-atlas)
where the feed went.
