title: Null Island
tagline: Stops in the Gulf of Guinea.
---

0,0 is a point in the Atlantic, off the coast of West Africa, where every
missing coordinate goes to live. Its neighbours are the stops whose latitude
and longitude were swapped, which land a continent away from the rest of their
network.

## The law

A stop within 1 km of 0,0, or a stop more than 2,000 km from the middle of the
feed that would sit right next to it with latitude and longitude swapped back.
Only stops served by a trip whose service still runs are judged.

## How to get pardoned

Give every stop its real coordinates. If a stop has none, it should not be in
stops.txt.
